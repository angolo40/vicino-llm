    About VicinoLLM

    Local OpenAI-compatible LLM server on Android. Point any OpenAI client at and chat offline.

    Support

    VicinoLLM is free. If it helps you, a small tip keeps it going. No accounts, no KYC — just scan the QR or copy the address.
